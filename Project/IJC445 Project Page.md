
# IJC455: Exploring Female Artists on Billboard Hot 100 
**Visualising the influence of women on the billboard Hot 100 songs list**

## Research Questions
  RQ1: How many artists in the top 100 artist list are women? 
  
  RQ2: Which of the female singers have the highest number of songs on the Billboard
  
  RQ3: How many songs did the top 3 female singers have ont he Billboard each year?
  
  RQ4: How on average do the top 3 singers songs look from a technical aspect?

## Methodology
- Data Loading and EDA
- Feature engineering
- Data Visualization


## Key Findings
- Female artists represent only 32% of the unique songs on the Hot 100 list
- Rihanna Beyonce and Taylor swift are the top 3 female artists across the period data was collected.
- Peak perfomance for each artist in terms of most songs on Billboard is: Rihanna - 2012, Beyonce - 2009 and Taylor Swift - 2015.
- All three artist widely produce loud danceable tracks while Beyonce has more speech-Heavy hits.
